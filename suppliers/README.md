# Audio Supplier Reference Library

The active supplier-source scope in this working store is **Audio & Entertainment only**.

## Retained supplier material
- `Audio Connections/` — STARSOUND and Sony Mobile ES audio catalogue and price-list references.

Audio supplier research and review-only product records may also be retained in the curated `catalogue-review/` and `catalogue-source/audio/` workspaces. Raw supplier sources must remain separate from customer-facing catalogue records.

## Handling rules
- Keep audio supplier documents in the matching audio supplier folder.
- Do not add ASC, Electrocity, Caelex, or non-audio supplier catalogues to the active working tree.
- Supplier documents are reference material only. Do not import prices, stock, products or catalogue changes into production without explicit owner approval.
- Do not store passwords, API keys, customer personal information or other secrets in this public repository.
- Historical commits remain available for recovery; do not restore retired supplier catalogues into the current tree.
