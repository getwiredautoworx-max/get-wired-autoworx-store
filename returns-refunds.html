3. Discretionary / change-of-mind refunds

Get Wired AutoWorx does not provide an unrestricted general right to return goods simply because a customer changed their mind, ordered incorrectly, no longer requires an item or later decides that an item is unsuitable, unless a statutory right applies.

Where Get Wired AutoWorx agrees to accept a discretionary refund that is not required by law, the item must be:

  • in its original condition;
  • in its original packaging fit for resale;
  • complete with all supplied accessories, fittings, manuals, labels and components; and

A 25% handling / restocking fee will be deducted from discretionary refunds where a charge is legally permitted.

The customer is also responsible for the return courier cost. The 25% fee does not apply where the CPA, ECTA or another mandatory law prohibits that deduction.

Statutory remedies take precedence over this discretionary refund policy.
