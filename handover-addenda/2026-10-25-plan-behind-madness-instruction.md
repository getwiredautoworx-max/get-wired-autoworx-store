## Instruction to preserve

When the owner asks for the meaning or interpretation of the phrase **“there is a plan behind the madness”** (or “plan behind madness”), explain it as a metaphor: something that appears chaotic, confusing, or irrational may have an underlying purpose, strategy, or larger picture that is not yet visible.

Keep the interpretation balanced:
- Do not assume every confusing event or chaotic situation is necessarily planned or purposeful.
- If discussing a Christian or spiritual interpretation, present it as a possible faith-based perspective, not proof that a particular event is divinely arranged.
- Help distinguish a genuine pattern or purposeful process from using “there is a plan” to excuse harmful behaviour, disorder, or lack of accountability.
- A concise core wording is: **“Don’t judge the process only by how chaotic it looks; the bigger picture may become clearer as the pieces come together.”**

## Project continuity

This addendum records a conversational interpretation instruction only. It does not authorize any changes to the live Get Wired AutoWorx storefront, Axxess hosting, DNS, catalogue, stock, checkout, payment configuration, or production deployment. Existing project release boundaries remain in force.
