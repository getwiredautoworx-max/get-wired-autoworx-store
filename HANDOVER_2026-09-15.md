## SUPPLIER SALESMAN QUOTE WORKFLOW / CUSTOMER QUERY-TO-PAY — 1 OCTOBER 2026
- New requested capability: the Owner has authorised login access to supplier sites, but some suppliers do not publish prices. Pricing and availability must be requested directly from a supplier salesman.
- Customer-facing workflow to design incrementally into the existing store:
  1. Customer opens a product and selects **Query Price & Availability** instead of a normal fixed-price purchase when applicable.
  2. Store creates a unique quote/request ID and captures the customer's requested product, exact store SKU/public SKU, quantity, vehicle/application details where relevant, customer name, email, WhatsApp/phone, delivery location and any other information needed by the supplier.
  3. System checks existing own-stock/product data first and identifies the appropriate authorised supplier route.
  4. Supplier query is sent to the designated salesman using the supplier's permitted communication channel (preferably an official API/feed/email integration; otherwise an authorised email/WhatsApp/manual-salesman workflow). Supplier website login credentials must remain private and must not be exposed to customers.
  5. Request should ask for: supplier price/cost, stock/availability, lead time, estimated dispatch date, packaging requirements/charges, delivery/freight cost or quotation, collection option if available, applicable VAT, minimum order quantities, warranty/return conditions where relevant, and quote validity/expiry.
  6. Supplier response is attached to the quote/request record and the system presents the Owner with a structured review/approval screen before any customer price is committed.
  7. After Owner approval, the system calculates the customer selling price according to the approved business pricing rule. Default store rule remains supplier cost × 1.15 VAT × 1.35 markup unless a specific approved quote rule is required for freight/packaging or exceptional supplier terms.
  8. Customer receives a complete quotation by email and/or WhatsApp containing product details, quantity, quoted selling price, VAT treatment, packaging charges, delivery/freight charges, expected lead time, total payable amount, quote validity/expiry, relevant conditions, and a clear acceptance/payment action.
  9. If customer accepts, generate a secure payment link through an approved payment provider. Do not collect or store card/bank credentials in the Get Wired application. Payment status must be verified from the payment provider before treating the order as paid.
  10. After verified payment, create/confirm the order, reserve or re-check supplier stock as appropriate, issue supplier purchase instructions where authorised, and send customer confirmation with order/reference number and subsequent delivery/tracking information when available.
- Quote lifecycle should be auditable: REQUESTED → SENT TO SUPPLIER → SUPPLIER RESPONSE → OWNER REVIEW → QUOTED TO CUSTOMER → ACCEPTED/PAYMENT PENDING → PAID → SUPPLIER ORDERED → DISPATCHED → DELIVERED / EXPIRED / DECLINED / CANCELLED.
- Each quote must have immutable timestamps, supplier reference, customer reference, product/SKU, quantity, supplier response, price components, delivery/packaging components, quote expiry and payment/order status.
- Customer must never see supplier cost, supplier margin, salesman contact details unless deliberately included by the Owner, supplier login information, or internal procurement notes.
- Supplier response should never be treated as confirmed stock indefinitely. Availability should carry a timestamp and, where possible, be re-confirmed immediately before payment/order submission when the supplier's stock is volatile.
- WhatsApp integration should use an authorised business/API channel rather than unofficial automation that could violate WhatsApp/provider terms. Email should use a controlled business email integration.
- Payment links must be single-purpose, HTTPS-secured, tied to the specific quote/order amount and expiry, and invalidated/reconciled when the quote expires, is cancelled or materially changes.
- Delivery and packaging must be separated from product price in the internal calculation so the customer can see a transparent final total without exposing supplier cost or markup formula.
- The Owner APK should provide: incoming customer queries, supplier query status, salesman response capture, quote builder, margin/price calculation, approve/send quote, payment status, supplier order status and customer communication history.
- Automation must not send a final customer price until a valid supplier response has been received and the required Owner approval rules have passed, unless a supplier integration later provides a formally approved automated-pricing feed.
- This workflow is intended for suppliers where the website requires authenticated access but does not display prices. Do not bypass CAPTCHA, anti-bot, access controls, or supplier terms. Prefer authorised supplier APIs/feeds and permitted communication channels.
- This is an incremental extension of the current Get Wired AutoWorx architecture. Do not rebuild the storefront or replace Supabase as the source of truth.
- Implementation prerequisite: identify the first supplier and its permitted salesman-query method (email, WhatsApp Business/API, portal messaging, or API) and then implement the generic quote/request engine so additional suppliers can be added without redesigning the customer workflow.
