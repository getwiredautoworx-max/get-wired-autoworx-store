## SUPPLIER SALESMAN QUOTE WORKFLOW
- Customer can request supplier pricing/availability where applicable.
- Supplier cost remains private.
- Supplier responses require Owner approval before a customer price is committed unless a formally approved automated supplier feed later permits otherwise.
- Quote lifecycle: REQUESTED → SENT TO SUPPLIER → SUPPLIER RESPONSE → OWNER REVIEW → QUOTED TO CUSTOMER → ACCEPTED/PAYMENT PENDING → PAID → SUPPLIER ORDERED → DISPATCHED → DELIVERED / EXPIRED / DECLINED / CANCELLED.
- Customer quotations must separate product, packaging and delivery/freight components without exposing supplier cost or internal margin data.
- Payment links must be generated only through an approved payment provider and verified against the specific quote/order.
- WhatsApp automation must use an authorised Business/API channel; email must use a controlled business integration.
