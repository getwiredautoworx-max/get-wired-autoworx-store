This policy explains how Get Wired AutoWorx handles personal information when you browse our website, request a quotation, contact us, or place an order. We aim to handle personal information responsibly in line with applicable South African privacy law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery/collection details, order items and notes, quotation requests, payment-status references, and messages you choose to send us. Basic technical information may also be processed by our hosting or security providers.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to questions, fitment enquiries and quotation requests.
  • To verify products, process orders, arrange collection or delivery, and provide customer support.
  • To confirm payment status, prevent misuse and maintain transaction records.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the website and meet legal or accounting obligations.

3. Sharing with service providers

We share only information reasonably needed for a task with relevant service providers, such as website/database hosting, payment providers, couriers, suppliers handling a special order, or professional advisers where required. Their own privacy terms may also apply. We do not ask customers to submit banking passwords, card PINs or one-time passwords through our order form or WhatsApp.

4. Payments

Payment is handled using the payment method presented at checkout. While payment-provider approval is pending, customers must follow only the instructions actually shown by Get Wired AutoWorx. Do not send full card details or banking credentials in order notes, email or WhatsApp.

5. Cookies and local storage

The storefront may use browser local storage to keep your shopping cart on your device. Hosting, security or embedded third-party tools may use their own technical storage or cookies. Clearing browser storage may remove your saved cart. Third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to handle enquiries/orders, provide support, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or further information about personal information we hold, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain records where lawfully required. Contact getwiredautoworx@gmail.com.

8. Children

The store is intended for people able to make purchase enquiries and transactions under applicable law. Please do not submit another person's personal information unless you are authorised to do so.

9. Collection and returns contact

Collection is by appointment only. No walk-ins and no cash on delivery (C.O.D.). Customers may collect only after payment has been received and Get Wired AutoWorx has notified them in writing that the order is ready. The returns address is kept private and is provided to the buyer in writing when a return is authorised or required.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The version displayed on this page applies from its stated update date.
