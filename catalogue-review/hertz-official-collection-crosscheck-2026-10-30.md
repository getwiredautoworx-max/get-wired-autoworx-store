## Remaining unresolved items

- The current official collections do not establish exact detail-page matches for every staged item. In particular, older CS 200/250/300 S-series drivers, DS 25.3 / DS 30.3, several standalone grilles, and HRC remote/controller accessories still need exact product-level checks.
- Two AFBMW harness/adapter rows remain unverified against a matching official model page.
- A collection listing is not sufficient to assign a primary product image or publish a product. No image URL has been downloaded, visually checked against the exact staged model, or approved in this pass.
- The separate 89-row Hertz workbook contains confidential dealer cost and regional stock snapshots. None of those values are reproduced here or authorised for public repository/customer display.
- Do not assign GW SKUs from Hertz item codes. The existing image matching rules still require an exact internal product/public_sku mapping, visual verification, and final asset existence.
