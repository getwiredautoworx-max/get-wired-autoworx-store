# 04. Returns, Refunds, and Warranty

## Handling returns
- Follow the current customer-facing Returns / Refunds page and Terms and Conditions. If those pages conflict with this note, stop and reconcile the wording before launch.
- The owner has requested that return shipping be paid by the buyer, returned goods be in original condition and original packaging suitable for resale, and a 25% handling fee apply to refunds. Confirm these clauses are legally appropriate and clearly disclosed before relying on them.
- Special-order items must not be represented as freely refundable after supplier payment. Obtain the required customer approval before ordering and follow applicable consumer law.
- Electrical/electronic claims may require installation evidence and a diagnostic report, subject to applicable law and the published warranty terms.

## Warranty
- Never promise a warranty outcome or duration that has not been confirmed for the specific product and supplier.
- Record the product/public SKU, order, installation details, fault report, supplier response, and resolution.
- Do not imply that supplier/manufacturer responsibility removes any statutory obligations of the retailer.

## Escalation
- Ambiguous, disputed, or potentially statutory consumer-rights cases require owner review before refusing a remedy or charging a fee.
