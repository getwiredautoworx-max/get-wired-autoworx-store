# 05. Product Inquiries and Escalation

## Information to collect
- Vehicle make, model, year, and 12V/24V system; engine or variant where relevant.
- Existing part number, connector photos, dimensions, and intended use.
- Requested quantity and delivery destination/option.

## Rules
- Do not guess compatibility from a similar image or partial product name.
- Match supplier SKU and part number to source evidence before confirming fitment.
- Missing or stale supplier prices mean quotation-only and inactive until verified.
- Do not expose supplier SKU or supplier cost in public listings or customer-facing APIs.
- If evidence is insufficient, say fitment or price needs confirmation rather than promising a match.

## Escalation
Escalate unclear electrical specifications, safety-critical use, conflicting part numbers, supplier substitutions, and missing warranty information.
