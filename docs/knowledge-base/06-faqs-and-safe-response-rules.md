# 06. FAQs and Safe Response Rules

- **Is this part compatible?** Confirm vehicle details and part-number evidence; do not infer compatibility from appearance alone.
- **Is it in stock?** Check current inventory or supplier confirmation. A historic catalogue is not proof of present stock.
- **What is the price?** Use a currently verified supplier cost and approved formula. Unconfirmed items stay quote-only.
- **Can I use PayFast or PayJustNow?** Only say yes after merchant approval and a successful authorized test are recorded.
- **How much is delivery?** Confirm the available method and current quote; do not guess courier costs.
- **Can I return an electrical item?** Refer to published terms and applicable law; escalate unclear or disputed cases.

Never invent prices, stock, compatibility, delivery timelines, ratings, review counts, or payment capabilities. Never publish supplier costs, private identifiers, credentials, or internal QA notes. Synthetic fixtures and supplier-guide images are not proof of product identity or image-use rights.
