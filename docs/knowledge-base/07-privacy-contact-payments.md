# 07. Privacy, Contact, and Payments

## Business contact
- Email used in project materials: getwiredautoworx@gmail.com
- Public store URL: https://www.getwiredauto.co.za

## Payments
- Confirm payment provider status from merchant-account evidence and an authorized test, not from a UI label or generated document.
- PayFast and PayJustNow must not be described as active until approval, required policy URLs, and safe end-to-end evidence are recorded.
- Never store card details or payment secrets in the repository, handover, browser code, or screenshots. Use provider-approved secure flows and authorized secret storage.

## Privacy and security
- Before launch, reconcile the published privacy notice with actual data collection, storage, processors, retention, and customer rights.
- Limit access to customer information and do not put personal customer data in public commits, synthetic fixtures, or issue comments.
- Escalate suspected exposure, security incidents, and requests involving personal data to the owner.
